## Requirements
### Requirement: Professional Relationships

Every staff member SHALL represent an ongoing professional relationship that MAY begin and end throughout a Career. Staff appointments and departures SHALL be recorded, and historical relationships SHALL be preserved as part of the golfer's history.

#### Scenario: Appointments and departures are recorded

- **WHEN** a staff member is appointed or departs
- **THEN** the appointment or departure SHALL be recorded in the golfer's staff history

### Requirement: Staff Changes Preserve History

Professional Golfers MAY replace existing staff members. Changing staff SHALL NOT invalidate Career history: former staff relationships SHALL remain permanently recorded, and the current Support Team SHALL be accurately maintained.

#### Scenario: Replacing staff keeps the former relationship on record

- **WHEN** a golfer replaces a staff member in a role
- **THEN** the former relationship SHALL remain in history and the current team SHALL reflect the new member

### Requirement: Continuity and Narrative

Professional relationships SHOULD develop over time and contribute to Career narrative (for example a long-serving coach or a mid-career coaching change), and the simulation SHALL recognise continuity without requiring permanent staff appointments.

#### Scenario: Long-serving relationships are visible in history

- **WHEN** a staff relationship spans multiple seasons
- **THEN** its duration SHALL be recoverable from the golfer's staff history, contributing to career narrative
