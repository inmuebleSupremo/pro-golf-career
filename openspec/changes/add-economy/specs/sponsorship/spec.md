## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sponsorship Agreements

Professional Golfers MAY enter Sponsorship Agreements — commercial relationships independent of Tournament participation that may provide financial rewards, objectives, reputation, and future opportunities. Sponsorships SHALL be tracked independently, their history SHALL be preserved, and multiple concurrent agreements SHALL be supported where permitted.

#### Scenario: A golfer signs an independent sponsorship

- **WHEN** a golfer accepts a sponsorship offer
- **THEN** a Sponsorship Agreement SHALL be tracked independently of Tournament results, providing its financial rewards, and recorded in sponsorship history

#### Scenario: Multiple agreements are supported

- **WHEN** a golfer holds more than one active agreement within the permitted limit
- **THEN** each SHALL be tracked independently

### Requirement: Sponsorship Objectives

Sponsorship Agreements MAY include performance objectives aligned with the golfer's competitive Career (for example participation, ranking achievements, tournament victories, seasonal consistency, or career milestones). Where an agreement carries objectives, they SHALL be evaluated against the golfer's competitive performance, and their success or failure SHALL be able to influence future Sponsorship opportunities.

#### Scenario: Objectives are evaluated against performance

- **WHEN** a golfer's season is evaluated against an agreement's objectives
- **THEN** each objective SHALL be marked met or unmet from the golfer's competitive performance, and success SHALL pay its reward

#### Scenario: Outcomes influence future opportunities

- **WHEN** an agreement's objectives are met or failed
- **THEN** that outcome SHALL influence whether the relationship renews or concludes

### Requirement: Reputation-Gated Opportunities

Commercial opportunities SHALL consider a golfer's professional reputation (rankings, tournament success, career achievements, historical consistency). Commercial reputation SHALL broadly reflect competitive reputation without being identical.

#### Scenario: Stronger reputation yields stronger offers

- **WHEN** offers are generated for a higher-reputation golfer versus a lower-reputation golfer
- **THEN** the higher-reputation golfer SHALL, broadly, receive more or more valuable offers

#### Scenario: Commercial reputation tracks but is not identical to competitive reputation

- **WHEN** commercial reputation is derived from competitive reputation
- **THEN** it SHALL broadly reflect competitive standing without being an exact copy of it

### Requirement: Sponsorship Continuity

Sponsorship relationships SHALL evolve throughout a Career: new opportunities SHALL emerge over time, and existing agreements MAY conclude, so commercial relationships reflect the golfer's evolving Career.

#### Scenario: Relationships begin and conclude over a career

- **WHEN** a Career progresses across seasons
- **THEN** new sponsorship opportunities SHALL be able to emerge and existing agreements SHALL be able to conclude
