## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Premium Links full-stage presentation

The web hole renderer SHALL select Premium Links for Links-family Generator 8 holes with principled
architecture provenance and valid canonical geometry. Older or unsupported Links holes SHALL retain the
polished Links renderer.

#### Scenario: Supported Links hole in normal play

- **WHEN** a supported Links hole is shown in the normal play stage
- **THEN** it SHALL render the approved vector source's top-down full-stage dunes, fescue, scrub,
  lighting and authentic bunker polygons, with smoothly capped nested playing-surface bands
- **AND** aiming, shot playback, and hazard depiction SHALL use canonical geometry and backend results.

#### Scenario: Decorative neighbouring property

- **WHEN** there is enough stage space to show neighbouring golf property
- **THEN** any neighbouring surface SHALL come from existing generated Links-hole geometry at the active
  hole's projection scale
- **AND** each visible neighbour SHALL use the same continuous rounded green-end bands as the active Links hole
- **AND** each visible neighbour SHALL have an outside walking route joined to the active access network;
  an unconnectable placement SHALL be retried or omitted
- **AND** decorative property SHALL not receive pointer input or become an aimable gameplay surface.
- **AND** all walking routes SHALL clear active and neighbouring playable surfaces, rounded outer bands,
  bunkers and water.

#### Scenario: Unsupported Links save

- **WHEN** the hole lacks Generator 8 principled provenance or valid canonical geometry
- **THEN** the established polished Links presentation SHALL remain selected.
