## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Organic route geometry remains playable

The system SHALL keep each V5 route connected from tee to green and SHALL retain a playable canonical corridor through all generated routing forms.

#### Scenario: Dogleg remains targetable

- **WHEN** a player or AI selects a normal progression target on a generated V5 dogleg
- **THEN** the target SHALL resolve to the authoritative playable corridor
- **AND** it SHALL use the same targeting rules as every other shot
