## Why

The active V4 Parkland generator emits bare corridors: its canonical policy does not create `TREES` terrain and its low water bias resolves to bunker-only hazards. Renderer material work cannot make absent authoritative features playable or visible.

## What Changes

- Selectively restore the deterministic V5 organic-hole architecture generator for newly created worlds.
- Use its route-aware fairway widths, irregular green footprints, tree enclosures and bunker complexes in canonical geometry.
- Retain V1--V4 generation for restored worlds and exclude the rejected V6 course-scale landscape placement prototype.
- Produce a small, repeatable multi-seed Parkland preview using genuine V5 backend geometry in the live canonical renderer before broader work.

## Non-goals

- Changing historical saved-world layouts, shot physics, player/AI rules, GraphQL contracts, or adopting V6.
- Copying any authored SVG hole as a procedural template.
