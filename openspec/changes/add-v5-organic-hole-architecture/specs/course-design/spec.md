# course-design Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: V5 course architecture identity composes independent tendencies

A V5 course SHALL derive a deterministic architecture profile distinct from biome, temporary setup and the existing
design profile. The profile SHALL use composable tendencies for routing movement, landing-width rhythm, approach
openness, green defence and biome-plausible recovery/hazard mix. It SHALL constrain candidate generation and
whole-course selection, not act solely as a name or visual label.

#### Scenario: Architecture identity has measurable course-wide effect

- **WHEN** two V5 courses have materially different architecture-profile dimensions
- **THEN** their selected-plan or quality-report distributions SHALL show at least one corresponding routing,
  fairway, approach, green or supported-hazard difference

### Requirement: V5 composition assesses diversity across all eighteen holes

Before V5 terrain compilation, its composition process SHALL evaluate routing, fairway geometry, landing/approach,
green form, hazard/recovery and difficulty distribution across the complete candidate selection. It SHALL preserve
existing par/length/archetype constraints while preventing excessive near-repetition; it SHALL not prescribe a
fixed recurring sequence of layout templates.

#### Scenario: V5 course contains structural variety

- **WHEN** a V5 course quality report is generated
- **THEN** it SHALL report its routing, width/asymmetry, landing/approach, green and repetition measures for all
  eighteen selected holes
- **AND THEN** the course SHALL satisfy its approved diversity and playability thresholds
