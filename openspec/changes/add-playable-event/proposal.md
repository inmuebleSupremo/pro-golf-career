## Why

`add-playable-round` built the interactive shot-by-shot round (`sim.play.PlayableRound`) and proved it byte-identical to the AI's `resolveRound`, but it is standalone — nothing drives it and nothing counts. The player's real tournaments still auto-resolve; the human never actually plays a round that feeds the ranking, their career, prize money, or the record book. This change **wires the played round into the player's live tournament so it counts**: when the player's scheduled event comes up the World *yields*, the player plays (or sims) their four rounds while the rest of the field auto-resolves around them, and on completion the cut, leaderboard, prizes, ranking, career, media, statistics, economy, and health all apply exactly as they do for an auto-resolved event. This is the payoff of "playing the golf" — the first end-to-end moment where a human decision on the course changes the world. It is the proof the game is real (player-experience §5, acceptance test #2).

Same engine for human and AI (REQ-104): the AI field resolves through the unchanged path, and the player substitutes their own decisions into the identical per-shot seams. The guardrail is fidelity — a player who sims every shot produces a tournament result byte-identical to today's automatic resolution.

## What Changes

- Add **`com.progolf.sim.play.PlayableEvent`**: an interactive tournament session that plays a real `Tournament` for the field with the player as an interactive competitor. Round by round it builds the player's `PlayableRound` (18 holes from the real course + that round's conditions, seeded identically to the automatic path), lets the human play or sim it, feeds the resulting round score into the Tournament, and advances the Tournament that round (auto-resolving every other competitor). It runs the cut over the combined field, and if the player ties for the lead after 72 holes it plays the **sudden-death playoff interactively** (the player plays each playoff hole, the tied rivals auto-resolve), always skippable.
- Modify **`sim.tournament.Tournament`** to accept **one interactive competitor** whose per-round score is supplied externally (`designateInteractiveCompetitor` + `submitInteractiveRoundScore`) instead of being computed by the shared resolver, and to expose interactive sudden-death (`beginPlayoff` / `playoffContenders` / `submitPlayoffHole` / per-rival playoff-hole resolution) so the orchestrator can pace it. Every non-interactive competitor resolves through the unchanged path; with no interactive competitor designated, Tournament behaviour is unchanged.
- Modify **`sim.world.World`** to **yield at the player's event**: `advanceWeek` resolves this week's events in order, but when it reaches an event the player is entered in it builds a `PlayableEvent`, stores it as the *pending player event*, and pauses (health recovery, seasonal transition, and the calendar do not advance until the event completes). `resolveEvent` is refactored so its "build the event" and "feed every consumer" halves are shared by the automatic and interactive paths; on `completePlayerEvent()` the feed-consumers half runs on the player's real result and the paused week resumes (remaining events, then recovery/transition/advance). `advanceSeason` auto-sims any pending player event so bulk advance still runs the world.
- Expose the capability at the app seam: **`WorldService`** gains methods to detect the pending event, read the current shot situation and live leaderboard, play/sim a shot, hole, round, or the whole event, and complete it.

Explicitly out of scope: no change to shot mathematics, `resolveShot`, `resolveRound`, or the cut/prize/ranking rules; no GraphQL or REST endpoints (the provisional REST is superseded by the later GraphQL slice — this change wires the engine + `WorldService` only); no event *prestige/majors* (the next engine slice); no create-your-golfer. The player still controls the golfer designated by `add-player-control`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `playable-event`: The player's live tournament played interactively — the human plays or sims their four rounds (and any sudden-death playoff) while the rest of the field auto-resolves around them, and the completed result counts toward the cut, leaderboard, prizes, ranking, career, media, statistics, economy, and health exactly as an auto-resolved event; a fully-simmed player event is byte-identical to automatic resolution.

### Modified Capabilities
- `tournament-play`: the Tournament engine additionally supports exactly one interactive competitor whose per-round (and per-playoff-hole) score is supplied externally rather than computed by the shared resolver, with all other competitors and all cut/leaderboard/playoff/completion rules unchanged.
- `world-progression`: when a world has a designated player, advancing a week *yields* at that player's scheduled event — building a pending interactive event and pausing the week — and resumes (feeding every consumer from the real result) when the event completes; a bulk season advance sims the pending event, and a world with no designated player is unchanged.

## Impact

- **Codebase**: new `com.progolf.sim.play.PlayableEvent`; modified `Tournament` (interactive competitor + interactive playoff), `World` (yield/resume + `resolveEvent` split into build/feed halves + pending-event API), `WorldService` (drive the event). No shot-engine or scoring changes.
- **Fidelity/determinism**: the AI field resolves through the unchanged per-competitor path; the player's round uses the identical per-shot seed coordinates (`worldSeed, season, tournamentId, round, playerFieldIndex, hole, shot`) so a simmed `PlayableEvent` equals the automatic tournament result bit-for-bit — the core guardrail test. The interactive playoff reuses the same playoff-hole seeds as automatic sudden death.
- **DAG**: `play` now also depends on `tournament` (and the domains it already used); `world` composes `play`. Nothing lower imports `play` or `world`; the dependency direction is preserved.
- **Boundary/risk**: `World` remains a pure coordinator — it computes no scores; it drives the Tournament and the `PlayableEvent` and feeds their result to the owning domains. The pause/resume is explicit state on `World` (a nullable pending event), not a thread or coroutine.
