# Design

`docs/course-holes/links001.svg` supplies the visual benchmark: open fescue surround, low irregular fairway,
subdued green, pot-bunker clusters and dune-like material variation. V5 Links already supplies routes, rough,
fairway, greens, bunker polygons and occasional canonical shoreline water; its environment plan keeps tree
regions sparse. The frontend adds Links-specific material patterns within those regions and a muted, non-playable
dune backdrop outside the playable boundary. Neither creates a hazard footprint or gameplay interaction.

The accepted interim Parkland green complexes still have near-circular, concentric putting surfaces and angular
outer recovery boundaries. Those are future architecture refinements, not part of this Links visual checkpoint.
