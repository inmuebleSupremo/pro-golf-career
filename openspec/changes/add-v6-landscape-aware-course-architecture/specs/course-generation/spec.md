# course-generation Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: V6 is a retained landscape-aware generator

Course generation SHALL support retained V6 through an explicit generator pin. V6 SHALL compose shared landscape
and placements before V5-derived local architecture/canonical terrain. V1--V5 generated output SHALL remain exact,
and V6 SHALL not become the new-world current version without explicit adoption approval.

#### Scenario: V5 remains isolated

- **WHEN** a V5 fixture is generated after V6 exists
- **THEN** it SHALL retain exact V5 output and SHALL not receive V6 landscape provenance
