# course-design Specification

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: V6 course identity includes shared geography

V6 SHALL assess eighteen-hole composition using placement, transition and multi-hole environmental relationships in
addition to retained V5 local routing/landing/green diversity. Landscape conditions SHALL constrain route geometry,
playing corridors/clearings, landing areas, green placement/approach and truthful recovery/hazard opportunities
before final local candidate selection. V6 SHALL NOT select fixed hole templates.

#### Scenario: A clearing informs a landing area

- **WHEN** a V6 woodland placement intersects a suitable shared clearing
- **THEN** its selected local plan MAY use that clearing for an actionable canonical landing relationship
