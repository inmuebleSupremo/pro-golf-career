## Why

Generator 7 established a playable Parkland/Links visual baseline, but it is not evidence that its architectural variety produces genuine, geometry-backed target decisions. This change creates a bounded development preview to test that question without changing careers or the default generator.

## What Changes

- Add a deterministic, development-only preview path that derives varied route geometry and landing intent from continuous hole inputs rather than a selected named hole template.
- Compile its terrain through the existing canonical geometry and hazard systems, then derive target options through the shared strategic target evaluator.
- Export and render a complete, fixed Parkland/Links seed corpus with known and unseen samples in the existing playable-hole renderer.

## Non-Goals

- A new persisted generator version, default-generator change, existing-save migration, GraphQL contract, or gameplay-rule change.
- Course-scale placement, the rejected V6 landscape experiment, or a claim of final golf-course realism.

## Impact

- `sim.course` gains an isolated preview seam and tests; ordinary generators and saves retain their exact paths.
- The frontend gains a development-only renderer review route backed by exported canonical fixture data.
