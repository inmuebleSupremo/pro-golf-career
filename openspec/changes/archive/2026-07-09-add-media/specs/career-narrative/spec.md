## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Career Narrative

Every Professional Golfer SHALL develop an evolving Career Narrative that emerges from accumulated Career events (for example breakthrough season, consistent contender, veteran resurgence, dominant champion, or long championship drought). The Career Narrative SHALL be descriptive rather than prescriptive.

#### Scenario: A narrative emerges from accumulated events

- **WHEN** a golfer's career accumulates events over seasons
- **THEN** a descriptive Career Narrative SHALL be derivable from those events and SHALL evolve as the career changes

#### Scenario: The narrative is descriptive, not prescriptive

- **WHEN** a Career Narrative is derived
- **THEN** it SHALL describe the career without determining or altering future outcomes

### Requirement: Narrative Diversity

The Media System SHALL recognise different forms of success — for example tournament victories, career longevity, rising prospects, successful comebacks, and historic consistency — and SHALL NOT focus exclusively on championship winners.

#### Scenario: Non-champions are recognised

- **WHEN** narratives are derived across a diverse field
- **THEN** forms of success beyond winning championships (such as a rising prospect or a comeback) SHALL be recognisable

### Requirement: Personalisation

The Media System MAY prioritise information based on the player's Career, giving relevant events greater prominence. Prioritisation SHALL affect presentation only, and all World events SHALL remain equally valid within the simulation.

#### Scenario: Prioritisation does not change validity

- **WHEN** events are prioritised for a particular golfer
- **THEN** the ordering SHALL affect prominence only, and every World event SHALL remain equally valid
