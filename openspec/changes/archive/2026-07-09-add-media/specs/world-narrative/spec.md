## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Persistent World Narrative

The simulation SHALL maintain a persistent World Narrative that belongs to the World rather than any individual golfer and emerges from tournament history, career progression, rankings, retirements, and historical achievements.

#### Scenario: The world narrative accrues from world events

- **WHEN** tournaments are played, careers progress, rankings change, and golfers retire over time
- **THEN** the World Narrative SHALL accumulate the corresponding news as a persistent record belonging to the World

### Requirement: Player Awareness Beyond Direct Experience

The Media System SHALL communicate events beyond the player's direct experience — for example results from unattended tournaments, rival achievements, tour developments, and records — so the World continues to feel active regardless of player participation.

#### Scenario: Events the player did not attend are reported

- **WHEN** significant events occur that do not involve the player
- **THEN** the Media System SHALL still generate news for them

### Requirement: Historical Reporting

Historically significant events (for example major tournament victories, world number-one changes, career milestones, and record performances) SHALL remain discoverable after they occur, contributing to long-term World continuity.

#### Scenario: Significant past events remain discoverable

- **WHEN** a historically significant event has occurred in a past season
- **THEN** it SHALL remain discoverable in the media history afterward
