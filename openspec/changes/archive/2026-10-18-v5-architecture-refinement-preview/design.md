# Design

Refine V5 candidate movement and landing-width rhythm, then derive each canonical terrain edge independently while
keeping surface bands nested. Give greens par-sensitive playable size and varied orientation, with compact default
finishes rather than a generic widened cap. Reserve added space for an actual defended/recovery composition. Align
larger bunker groups with a chosen landing side, separate water/bunkers/tree masses in canonical space, and use a
shoreline polygon rather than an isolated water oval. Subtle out-of-play landscape texture supplies context without
pretending to be a playable hazard. Keep all shot, lie, pin and hazard authority in backend geometry.

The development preview pairs the same seed, biome and hole number in retained V5 and experimental version 7 for
Parkland and Links. This is not integration approval; visible quality remains subject to manual review.
