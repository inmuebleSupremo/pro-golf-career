## Why

The season calendar is generated by an even-spread formula: events are distributed uniformly across the weeks of each tier, "signature" merely tags the earliest events of a tier (so they cluster at week 1 instead of spotlighting the season), all four majors are dropped on the Elite tier at even intervals, and there are no rest weeks or season finale. The result is a flat, structureless year — every tour feels the same and a career never "breathes." v2 aims for tour realism: a designed cadence per tier where majors anchor distinct chapters, signature events are spotlighted, rest weeks punctuate the grind, and each tour closes with a Tour Championship.

## What Changes

- Replace the formulaic `generateSchedule` with a **tier-specific structured cadence** over the standard 30-week season: majors at fixed "chapter" weeks on Elite (≈7 / 14 / 21 / 27), signature events spotlighted (including a mid-season Elite+Development **collision** ≈week 17), intentional **rest weeks** (especially bracketing majors), and each tour carrying its own rhythm.
- Raise event density from 6 to a **moderate ~12–16 events per tier per season** — roughly every other week plus the structured peaks (not a near-weekly full calendar).
- Add a **Tour Championship** season-finale prestige, one per tour: **Elite week 30**, **Development week 29** (Development finishes a week early so newly promoted players get a break before their next season). It weights ranking points / prize / career legacy **above a Signature and below a Major**.
- Preserve **determinism** (seed-reproducible schedules) and **cross-tour majors** (a major's field is still the strongest golfers across all tiers).
- **Graceful degradation**: the designed cadence targets the 30-week standard scale; smaller `weeksPerSeason` configurations (the test worlds) fall back to a simple proportional schedule that still satisfies the schedule contract.
- **Non-goal (deferred):** a full playoff series (season-ending points reset / staged multi-event finale). This change ships only the single Tour Championship finale.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- _None — this change extends existing scheduling and prestige capabilities rather than introducing a new one._

### Modified Capabilities

- `world-schedule`: schedule generation gains a structured, tier-specific cadence — designed placement of majors (fixed chapter weeks), signature spotlights, rest weeks, and a per-tour season-finale championship, at a moderate density — replacing the even-spread formula. Determinism and cross-tour major fields are preserved; small-season configs degrade to a simple proportional schedule.
- `event-prestige`: introduce a **Tour Championship** prestige level, ranked above Signature and below Major, and extend the monotonic reward / closing-pressure / course-setup weighting to include it.

## Impact

- **Engine (`com.progolf.sim`):** `World.generateSchedule` (rewritten around a cadence definition); `EventPrestige` enum (+ `TOUR_CHAMPIONSHIP`); `ScheduledTournament`; `WorldConstants` / `WorldConfig` (density + cadence parameters). Reward weighting (prize + ranking points in `TourSystem` / standings), situational closing-round pressure, and course-setup difficulty tables all extended for the new prestige level.
- **Balance:** the ~2× density increase may require recalibration of world scoring, Hall-of-Fame score thresholds, and career-earnings expectations; season-simulation performance is validated at the new density (advancing must stay responsive over a multi-decade career).
- **Tests:** existing small-config schedule/progression tests (6-week seasons, `WorldConfig(40,6,3,20,4)`) must continue to pass via the degradation path; new tests assert the 30-week cadence (major weeks, rest weeks, championship placement, density, determinism).
- **Frontend / API:** none required — the schedule and prestige already surface through GraphQL; the new prestige contributes a display label only. Sim purity is preserved (all changes stay in `com.progolf.sim`).
