## Why

Tournaments already compute prize money — every `TournamentResult.Finish` carries a `prize` — but it evaporates: there is no wallet to pay it into, no expenses to weigh it against, and no commercial life around the golfer. A career today has a competitive arc (rank, tiers, aging) but no *financial* arc, so none of the money decisions that define a real professional career exist. Economy & Sponsorship is the layer that turns those prize amounts into a living financial identity: golfers bank winnings, pay to compete, sign sponsors with objectives to chase, and must balance ambition against sustainability as a career lengthens. It makes competitive success *pay* — and makes the back half of a career about more than just scores.

## What Changes

- Give every Professional Golfer a **Financial Identity** (REQ-177/178/182/184/189): a `FinancialAccount` tracking available funds, career earnings, tournament earnings, sponsorship income, and career expenses, backed by an **auditable transaction ledger** — every credit and debit is recorded, funds cannot be discretionarily overspent, and financial history (plus milestones) persists for the life of the career.
- **Award prize money automatically and charge career expenses** per event (REQ-178/182): tournament winnings are credited from the finish, and entry/travel expenses are debited, so results move the balance.
- Add **Sponsorship** (REQ-179/180/186/188): independent commercial agreements that pay income and carry **performance objectives** (participation, ranking, wins, consistency, milestones); offers are **reputation-gated** (commercial reputation broadly reflects competitive reputation without being identical); objectives are **evaluated** (success pays bonuses and aids renewal, failure ends the relationship); and agreements **begin and conclude** across a career, evolving with it — with sponsorship history preserved.
- Add **meaningful financial decisions** (REQ-181/183/187): a deterministic AI acceptance/management policy signs and renews sponsorships with real trade-offs (limited slots, objectives a golfer may not meet) rather than auto-optimising; financial opportunity broadly reflects competitive success; and longer careers face a genuine sustainability tension.
- **Wire the economy into the World** (modifies `world-progression`): the World gives every golfer a financial identity, awards prize money and charges expenses each event, and runs a **seasonal financial cycle** (pay sponsors, evaluate objectives, conclude/renew agreements, generate and accept new offers) — deterministically, keeping the world reproducible.

Explicitly out of scope, per REQ-185/190: the Economy **never modifies player attributes, tournament outcomes, rankings, or shot resolution** — financial success provides *opportunities*, it does not purchase competitive success — and it is **not responsible for staff, equipment, tournament scoring, player progression, or rankings** (those stay with their domains; Staff and Equipment are separate deferred domains). Also deferred: the human-facing UI for accepting offers and managing money (Presentation; a deterministic AI policy drives decisions for now), investment/expansion systems (REQ-181 "future expansion"), and any real-currency/tax modelling. The Economy is deterministic — prize and expense amounts are pure, and sponsorship offer generation draws from an isolated seed stream — so a seeded world stays reproducible.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `financial-identity`: A per-golfer `FinancialAccount` — available funds, career/tournament/sponsorship earnings and career expenses — backed by an auditable transaction ledger with economic integrity (no discretionary overspend; mandatory obligations follow a defined negative-balance rule), automatic prize awards, incurred expenses, and permanently preserved financial history and milestones (REQ-177/178/182/184/189).
- `sponsorship`: Independent sponsorship agreements providing financial rewards, performance objectives, and reputation; reputation-gated offer generation (commercial reputation broadly reflecting competitive reputation, not identical); objective evaluation (success bonuses, failure ends renewal); and agreements that begin and conclude as the career evolves, with sponsorship history preserved (REQ-179/180/186/188).
- `financial-strategy`: Meaningful financial decisions via a deterministic AI acceptance/management policy with strategic trade-offs (not auto-optimisation); financial opportunity that broadly reflects competitive success; long-term career sustainability; and the economic-independence boundary — the Economy provides opportunity but never modifies attributes, outcomes, rankings, or shot resolution (REQ-181/183/185/187/190).

### Modified Capabilities
- `world-progression`: The World gives every golfer a financial identity, awards prize money and charges career expenses per event, and runs a seasonal financial cycle (sponsor payments, objective evaluation, agreement conclusion/renewal, and reputation-gated offer generation and acceptance) — deterministically, keeping the world reproducible.

## Impact

- **Codebase**: New framework-free `com.progolf.sim.economy` package (`FinancialAccount`, `Transaction`/`TransactionType`, `SponsorshipAgreement`/`SponsorshipObjective`, `SponsorshipMarket`, `AcceptancePolicy`, `PerformanceSnapshot`, `FinancialMilestone`, `EconomyConstants`). `World` gains a `Map<String, FinancialAccount>` created at `admit`, per-event prize/expense wiring in `resolveEvent`, and a financial cycle in `seasonalTransition`.
- **Determinism**: Prize and expense amounts are pure functions of results/tier; sponsorship offer generation and commercial-reputation variation draw from an isolated per-golfer/season seed stream (via `Seeds`/`SplitMix64Rng`), so worlds stay reproducible and the acceptance policy is deterministic.
- **DAG**: `economy` depends only on `core` (RNG). The World feeds it primitives (prize amounts, a competitive-reputation score, a `PerformanceSnapshot` built from career/ranking), so `economy` imports no other domain; only `world` imports `economy`.
- **Downstream consumers (future changes)**: Staff and Equipment become spendable investments against the account; Media/news can surface earnings and big signings; Career legacy can fold in financial milestones; Presentation surfaces the wallet and offer decisions.
- **Boundary/risk**: the Economy is the first domain to hold spendable state with an integrity rule (no overspend, every transaction recorded, negative balances defined) — confined to `FinancialAccount` and asserted to never touch attributes/outcomes/rankings; the seasonal-cycle wiring touches the World, whose full-run reproducibility test guards against regressions.
