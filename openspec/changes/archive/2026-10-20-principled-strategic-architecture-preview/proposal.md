## Why

Generator 7 established a playable Parkland/Links visual baseline, but it did not establish that architectural variety produced genuine, geometry-backed target decisions. The bounded preview demonstrated an improved principles-driven foundation, was promoted as Generator 8 for new opt-in test careers, and completed manual gameplay E2E acceptance. It remains an accepted foundation rather than a claim of final golf-course realism.

## What Changes

- Retain the accepted principles-driven routing, landing intent, strategic bunkering, and environment-aware composition as deterministic Generator 8.
- Make Generator 8 available only through an explicit development opt-in for newly created careers; existing worlds and saved careers retain their persisted generator pins.
- Carry its route-derived strategic intent through the same backend-authoritative canonical geometry and target-evaluation path used in real rounds.
- Preserve the complete, fixed Parkland/Links review corpus as evidence, including known and unseen samples rendered through the existing playable-hole renderer.

## Non-Goals

- Default-generator adoption, existing-save migration, or changes to historical generator output.
- Course-scale shoreline geography, the rejected V6 landscape experiment, a renderer redesign, or a claim of final golf-course realism.
- Further landscape, hazard, environmental interaction, putting, or broader simulation work.

## Impact

- `sim.course` retains Generator 8 beside V1–V7; explicit version provenance keeps ordinary generators and saves exact.
- World creation, persistence, GraphQL, and Play Details expose the selected Generator 8 provenance without changing the normal default.
- The frontend retains the development review route backed by exported canonical fixture data.
