## Why

Every golfer in the world plays with the same invisible, unchanging set of clubs. There is no bag to build, nothing to upgrade as the winnings roll in, and no gear that makes a great putter putt better. The shot engine already resolves per club, and the Economy already has a `spend` waiting for a buyer — but no equipment fills either. Equipment & Tournament Loadout is the layer that turns a golfer's clubs into owned, chooseable, improvable kit: a starting bag everyone carries, upgrades bought as careers grow, and a prepared loadout whose characteristics the shot engine actually feels. It gives the money another meaningful outlet and makes preparation — not just play — part of the game.

## What Changes

- Give every Professional Golfer a persistent **Equipment Inventory** (REQ-203/204): all owned equipment across the seven V1 categories (Driver, Fairway Woods, Hybrids, Irons, Wedges, Putter, Golf Ball), with ownership recorded and the inventory persisting throughout the Career.
- Model **equipment characteristics** (REQ-207): each item carries gameplay characteristics (forgiveness, power, workability, feel); the specification defines their existence, and V1 applies forgiveness and power to play.
- Prepare a **Tournament Loadout** and derive a **Golf Bag** (REQ-205/206/211): before play a loadout is selected from owned equipment, a Golf Bag is derived from it, and **the shot engine consumes the Golf Bag** — one active bag per entry, always valid, always owned, fixed during play. Integrity is maintained between inventory, loadout, and bag.
- Feed the bag into **shot resolution** (REQ-206/207): the active bag's aggregate forgiveness tightens dispersion and its power extends reach — a modest effect so better gear helps without buying wins — synced into shots the same way physical fatigue already is. Standard gear is neutral, reproducing current play exactly.
- Support **acquisition** integrated with the Economy (REQ-210): golfers may **purchase** equipment upgrades over a Career through their `FinancialAccount`, via a deterministic policy gated by affordability; significant equipment changes and ownership are preserved as history (REQ-209), contributing to a golfer's long-term identity.
- **Wire equipment into the World** (modifies `world-progression`, `shot-resolution`, `player-entity`): the World gives each golfer a starting inventory and loadout, runs Economy-integrated acquisition each season, syncs the active bag's characteristics into shot resolution before play, validates loadouts, and records ownership history — deterministically, keeping the world reproducible.

Explicitly out of scope, per REQ-214: the Equipment domain is **not responsible for shot calculations, financial transactions, tournament scoring, or player progression** — it owns ownership/inventory/loadouts/bag/preparation, exposes characteristics the shot engine reads (it does not compute shots), and never modifies attributes or results directly. Also deferred (REQ-213 future extensibility): equipment condition/wear, club fitting, manufacturer relationships, cosmetics, additional categories, sponsorship-supplied equipment, and per-club (rather than bag-aggregate) shot effects — the model is built so these add without fundamental change. Equipment acquisition is deterministic — drawn from an isolated seed stream — so a seeded world stays reproducible.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `equipment-inventory`: A persistent per-golfer Equipment Inventory across the seven V1 categories, with recorded ownership, preserved ownership history for significant changes, and acquisition (purchase) integrated with the Economy — built to support future expansion (REQ-203/204/209/210/213).
- `tournament-loadout`: A Tournament Loadout prepared before play and a Golf Bag derived from it that the shot engine consumes — one active bag per entry, containing only owned equipment, kept consistent with the inventory and always valid, fixed during play, and prepared without altering completed history (REQ-205/206/208/211/212).
- `equipment-influence`: Equipment characteristics that modulate shot resolution through the active Golf Bag (forgiveness and power in V1), consistent with the domain boundary — equipment provides characteristics the shot engine reads and never directly modifies results, attributes, scoring, or finances (REQ-207/214).

### Modified Capabilities
- `shot-resolution`: A shot additionally consumes the golfer's active equipment characteristics (forgiveness reduces dispersion, power extends reach), carried on the golfer's temporary state and neutral by default — so standard equipment reproduces prior shot behaviour exactly.
- `player-entity`: A Player's temporary state additionally carries a transient equipment profile (forgiveness, power), set before play from the active bag and surfaced to the shot engine; it is not a permanent attribute and never persists into attributes.
- `world-progression`: The World gives each golfer an inventory and loadout, runs Economy-integrated equipment acquisition each season, syncs the active bag into shot resolution before play, validates loadouts, and records ownership history — deterministically, keeping the world reproducible.

## Impact

- **Codebase**: New framework-free `com.progolf.sim.equipment` package (`EquipmentCategory`, `EquipmentCharacteristics`, `EquipmentItem`, `EquipmentInventory`, `TournamentLoadout`, `GolfBag`, `EquipmentCatalogue`, `AcquisitionPolicy`, `EquipmentAcquisition`, `EquipmentConstants`). `GolferState` gains equipment fields (neutral default); `ShotResolver` applies them; `PlayerState` carries a transient equipment profile surfaced via `Player.toGolferState`. `World` gains inventories/loadouts, an acquisition step through the Economy, a bag→shot sync, and ownership history. One `TransactionType` (`EQUIPMENT_PURCHASE`) is added.
- **Determinism**: acquisition and upgrade generation draw from an isolated per-golfer/season seed stream; the shot effect is a pure factor; standard gear is exactly neutral, so pre-equipment behaviour and world reproducibility are preserved.
- **DAG**: `equipment` depends only on `core`. The World reads a bag's aggregate characteristics and syncs them into the player's transient state (a public op the shot engine already reads), and charges the Economy itself, so `equipment` imports no other domain; only `world` imports `equipment`.
- **Downstream consumers (future changes)**: per-club effects, condition/fitting, and sponsor-supplied equipment attach onto this model; Media/legacy can surface equipment identity; Presentation adds the loadout-builder UI.
- **Boundary/risk**: equipment is an ownership + characteristics layer, not a shot actor — it exposes characteristics the shot engine reads and never computes shots or writes attributes/results. The shot-resolution change is guarded by exact neutrality for standard gear and the world's full-run reproducibility and scoring-calibration tests.
